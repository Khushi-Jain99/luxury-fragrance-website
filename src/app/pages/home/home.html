Our meticulous process of creation

01

Ethical Sourcing

We work directly with local farmers in Grasse, Haiti, and Morocco to source the purest raw materials.

02

Artisanal Extraction

Using slow steam distillation and co2 extraction to preserve the delicate olfactory profile of each ingredient.

03

Master Blending

Each formula is aged for six months in stainless steel vats to allow the molecules to harmonize perfectly.

04

Signature Bottling

Hand-poured and inspected in our Paris atelier, ensuring every bottle meets our exacting standards.
