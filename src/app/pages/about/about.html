Our Story

A journey through scent, time, and emotion.

The Vision

Founded in the heart of Paris, Aurelia began as a small atelier dedicated to capturing the ephemeral beauty of nature. Our founder, a botanist turned perfumer, believed that every flower, wood, and resin had a story to tell—one that could only be narrated through the complex language of olfaction.

Today, we continue that tradition of excellence, working with independent growers globally to ensure that every drop of essence in our bottles is of the highest purity and ethical origin.

Minimalism & Art

We believe in the beauty of simplicity. Our bottles are designed with a minimal aesthetic to let the fragrance inside be the true protagonist. We avoid unnecessary additives, focusing instead on the raw power of the ingredients themselves.

Every Aurelia fragrance is a collaboration between science and art, a meticulous balance that results in a scent that evolves with your skin throughout the day.

Purity

No synthetic fillers. Only the finest botanical essences and pure grain spirits are ever used in our formulations.

Heritage

Drawing from centuries of French perfumery while embracing modern sustainable extraction methods.

Intimacy

Fragrance is personal. We design scents that create a private radius, meant to be discovered, not announced.

Our Commitment

Luxury must be responsible. We are committed to 100% plastic-free packaging by 2025 and ensure all our ingredient partners follow fair-trade practices. Our bottles are infinitely refillable at any of our global boutiques, encouraging a circular approach to beauty.

We believe that true elegance is found when the pursuit of beauty aligns with the preservation of our planet's most precious resources.
